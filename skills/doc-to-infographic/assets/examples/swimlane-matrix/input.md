# FP&A-Led M&A Diligence Playbook For A U.S. Commercial Bank Acquisition

## Purpose

This playbook helps Arvest FP&A lead the financial diligence work required to answer one decision question: should Arvest acquire a commercial-bank target at the proposed price and terms, given strategic fit, target assessment score, normalized earnings, credit/funding quality, regulatory path, integration complexity, synergy value capture, capital impact, EPS impact, TBV dilution and earnback, post-close run-rate tracking, and execution risk? [FRAMEWORK]

## Operating Principles

- Start with the acquisition thesis, no-go criteria, and decision gates.
- Treat target data as unproven until tied to source documents, regulatory filings, management reporting, and workstream owner sign-off.
- Keep a single integrated risk/opportunity register and a single model assumption log.
- Require model labels for every input: source-provided, public-source, model assumption, placeholder, or user-needed.
- FP&A owns the economic spine of the process: target assessment, model logic, assumption governance, synergy value capture, integration budget tracking, and post-close run-rate reporting.
- Do not treat regulatory approval, legal terms, credit marks, or capital outcomes as concluded until supported by counsel, advisors, and current data.

## Phase Gates

| Phase | Objective | Required Outputs | Decision Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Setup | Define thesis, FP&A role, team, timeline, and VDR protocol. | Workstream roster, calendar, initial request list, model shell, target scorecard. | Proceed to VDR review. |
| 1. Target Assessment | Score target fit and identify the diligence questions that most affect value. | Target assessment scorecard, initial financial bridge, preliminary issue list. | Continue, pause, or narrow diligence. |
| 2. Deep Diligence | Validate earnings, credit, deposits, compliance, technology, workforce, and synergies. | Workstream findings, updated model, risk register, assumption log. | Final-round diligence focus. |
| 3. Decision Package | Convert findings into bid/LOI support. | Final memo, IC/board summary, model outputs, unresolved item list. | Bid / no bid / reprice / defer. |
| 4. Signing-to-Close Planning | Prepare application support, closing, Day 1, value capture, and conversion plan. | Regulatory package inputs, integration plan, synergy baseline, Day 1 budget. | Close readiness. |
| 5. Post-Close Tracking | Compare actual run-rate economics, spend, and retention outcomes to the deal model. | Integration financial plan, synergy tracker, variance report, corrective action list. | Realize value or intervene. |

## Bank-Specific Diligence Spine

1. Regulatory path and public-interest factors: map filing requirements, competition, financial stability, financial/managerial resources, safety and soundness, community needs, and resolvability. [OCC-BIZCOMBO] [FDIC-APP] [FDIC-POLICY]
2. Credit and loan portfolio: stratify portfolio, run independent credit review, estimate credit marks and CECL/provision implications, and identify concentration risks. [BARRET] [FDIC-APP]
3. Deposit and funding quality: identify core, noninterest-bearing, uninsured, rate-sensitive, brokered, public, and concentrated deposits; estimate runoff and CDI. [FDIC-APP]
4. Compliance and supervisory readiness: review exam history, CMS, BSA/AML, CRA, fair lending, consumer compliance, UDAAP, complaints, and remediation. [FDIC-CMS] [OCC-BSP]
5. Technology, operations, and vendors: map core conversion and third-party risk from diligence through integration. [TPRM]
6. FP&A financial model: tie target assessment, target financials, normalized earnings, purchase accounting, synergies, integration costs, EPS, TBV earnback, capital, integration planning, and post-close tracking. [FRAMEWORK]
7. Revenue and workforce durability: test customer and employee NLE, key producer risk, incentive compatibility, and revenue dis-synergy. [FRAMEWORK]

## Decision Outputs

- Investment thesis and strategic fit.
- Target assessment scorecard and diligence prioritization.
- Normalized earnings and quality-of-earnings adjustments.
- Credit mark, deposit mark, CDI, goodwill, and purchase accounting assumptions.
- Standalone, base-case, downside, and upside pro forma financial model.
- Cost synergy, revenue synergy, revenue dis-synergy, synergy realization timing, integration cost, and retention cost schedules.
- EPS accretion/dilution, TBV dilution and earnback, capital ratio impact.
- Integration budget, post-close run-rate tracking baseline, and value-capture owner map.
- Regulatory, compliance, credit, technology, customer, workforce, and integration risk register.
- Final diligence memo with bid/no-bid/reprice/defer recommendation.
