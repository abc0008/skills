# mosaic 3C.4N — full inlaid brand set

This package contains the exact micro-tessera construction of 3C.4 with
3C.2's deep-navy base lettering. The blue `a·i` remains distinct while the
complete wordmark stays fully inlaid within the mineral tessera field.

## Master assets

- `mosaic-3c4n-primary.svg` — full-color master for light backgrounds
- `mosaic-3c4n-footer.svg` — vector master for footer-scale placement
- `mosaic-3c4n-monochrome.svg` — graphite and neutral-grey treatment
- `mosaic-3c4n-on-dark.svg` — reversed treatment for dark backgrounds

SVG is the source-of-truth format. Use it wherever the destination supports
vector artwork.

## PNG assets

### Primary

- 320, 640, 1280, and 2560 pixels wide

### Footer scale

- 240 and 480 pixels wide

### Monochrome

- 640 and 1280 pixels wide

### On dark

- 640 and 1280 pixels wide

The PNG files retain transparency between tesserae. Place the on-dark version
over `#121212` or another near-black surface.

## Color specification

- Deep navy: `#14385C`
- Pinnacle blue: `#0074C4`
- Reversed blue: `#8EC6E6`
- Frost lettering: `#EBEBEB`
- Dark surface: `#121212`
- Mineral field: `#D4D4D1`, `#C8CDD0`, `#BFC6CC`, `#D0D2D3`, `#C3C7C3`

## Clear space

Maintain clear space equal to one full logical tessera around the mark. Do not
crop into the surrounding field cells or recolor individual tesserae.
